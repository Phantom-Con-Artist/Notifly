# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Shorter plugin description (no change in how Notifly works).
- Fixes for building the quick capture app on Windows.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Alt+C quick capture from anywhere, even when Obsidian is closed.
- Live Markdown, autosave, drafts, and `[[` suggestions.
- Categories: Thought, Task and + to start, make your own by typing, built for thousands.
- Files and voice notes: PDFs, pictures, sound, video and any other file.
- Inbox as a bento grid with real previews, a category overview, and one switch back to a plain grid.
- Search by words, `#tags`, `cat:`, `file:` and `type:`.
- Quick capture app for Windows, macOS and Linux, released next to the plugin.
